# Combat presentation and walking — 2026-09-10

## Current behavior

- Full armed character sprites replace the rejected flat arm overlays. Player pistol, AR and shotgun use separate compact grips. Robot and scavengers hold their actual guns. Five base bearings plus mirroring give eight visual directions; projectile aim remains continuous.
- Eight generated walking sheets: three player loadouts and all five enemies. Four frames per bearing, advancing with actual traveled distance, reversed while retreating. Actors return to their idle pose when stopped. Dog uses a four-legged gait; robot and zombie have their own sheets. These are prototype generated cycles; no dedicated reload, throw, attack or death animation yet.
- Atlas loader keys magenta, detects animation row gaps, trims cells, filters once at gameplay size, and normalizes the scale across each cycle. The raw PNGs are retained unchanged. This is an engine import step.
- Distinct original procedural gun sounds combine noise transients, low body, mechanical action and tails. Four variations per weapon, slight pitch variation, stereo enemy positioning and distance falloff. Actual shot events avoid weapon-switch noise and cover all shooters. Cached samples, a 24-voice cap and master compressor bound mixing.
- HUD uses worn dark metal, muted amber accents, compact health/armor, an ammo panel, fire mode, magazine/reload progress, and a G/frag counter. Entry, field and death menus share the survival style. See [the subsequent interface and hit-feedback pass](survival-ui-and-hit-feedback.md). Keyboard and clickable weapon controls remain.
- G throws a grenade toward the cursor on the ground plane, maximum280 units. Three per run, .6sec flight, 1.4sec fuse, 115-unit radius, maximum120 damage falling linearly with distance. Armor absorbs first, including self-damage. Solid geometry stops throws and blocks blast damage. Grenades do not bounce or break cover in this prototype. Restart refills supplies.

## Sources and limitations

Original character identities come from our Cold Relay assets. [Scavland's official page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3373500/Scavland/) and the user's supplied gameplay image informed the compact grip and camera. No Scavland texture was imported into the game.

All raster assets below were created/edited with the built-in image-generation tool. Project paths are relative to public/art/cold-relay/. PNG source assets add approximately15 MB for this pass, before optimization. Local testing does not establish hardware GPU performance or online capacity. The pistol/AR/shotgun comparison WAV was rendered from the same sample generator and played through afplay; subjective audio approval remains with the user.
